## Overview

CareerLens is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry requirements.

The system automatically analyzes student resumes, extracts skills and certifications, evaluates career readiness, identifies missing competencies, and recommends suitable job roles along with personalized learning pathways.

Traditional resume screening is often manual, time-consuming, and inconsistent. CareerLens automates this process using NLP and Machine Learning, enabling students to make informed career decisions and improve employability.

---

## Problem Statement

Many students face challenges such as:

- Lack of personalized career guidance
- Unclear understanding of industry-required skills
- Poor resume optimization
- Difficulty identifying skill gaps
- Ineffective certification planning
- Uncertainty about placement readiness

Existing placement systems primarily focus on job listings rather than profile analysis and career development.

CareerLens addresses these challenges through intelligent resume analysis and AI-driven recommendations.
